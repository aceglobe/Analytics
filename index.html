Overview

What’s in each course

Course materials

Live Zoom Class Sessions, On-Demand Course videos, Hands-on labs and problem sessions, Course Project Work, Resume Clinics, LinkedIn Portfolio guide, Career Coaching and Interview Prep.

Learning flow

Two to four months live class sessions during weekends. Classes begin with foundational skills and build momentum to advanced level gradually. Participants are assigned groups for collaboration on class projects and assignments.

Soft Skills and Career Coaching

Our career coaches meet with participants weekly, providing guidance on LinkedIn portfolio building, Resume Prep and job application guide. Participants are also trained on interviewing skills and bargaining techniques for the job market.
